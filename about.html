Engineering Excellence

Pioneering Smart Automation & Liquid Dosing

ADIAN Solution & Services is an established B2B industrial engineering firm headquartered in West Bengal with manufacturing operations at Sankrail Industrial Park, Howrah. We specialize in designing, manufacturing, and deploying custom PLC panels, SCADA dashboards, PCC/MCC/APFC panel boards, and high-precision liquid dosing applicators.

Our mission is to eliminate unexpected manufacturing downtime and improve volumetric dosing accuracy for chemical processing, water treatment, pharmaceuticals, food batching, and heavy engineering industries.
